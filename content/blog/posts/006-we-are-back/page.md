Chair Flight has been stalled for roughly two years without any significant
development. We are finally back to active development, and we are working to
deliver new features.

## What has changed

Along the way we have already refactored the code to use up-to-date
technologies. We also moved our backend from Neon to Supabase, which gives us
room to grow and to introduce some very significant features.

We have also taken this opportunity to fully redesign the website, giving it a
fresher look and getting rid of some of the older artwork that has been
decorating Chair Flight for the last two years.

## What is coming: user progress

The feature we are most excited about is what we call user progress. It will
finally allow Chair Flight users to create an account, create tests and track
their progress, along with other features. Your tests will follow you across
devices, you will be able to analyse your statistics, take part in discussions
by commenting and replying, and even contribute to the Chair Flight question
banks directly through the app, without having to know anything about coding.

## A note on AI

We are now using AI tools to empower our code. Most of this refactor was done
with the help of Claude, and our new artwork was created with ChatGPT. We
believe that leveraging AI tools will allow us to keep up with the fast pace of
development in the market and to deliver the best possible code without
compromising on quality. Chair Flight is still managed and developed by
volunteers, and every code change is overseen by them.

## Free and open source

Our mission has not changed. Chair Flight remains **free and open source**, with
all questions and content available for you to navigate, download and study at
no cost.

That said, we are working towards a subscription model for the features tied to
user progress, which will help us cover the growing server costs of storing user
data remotely. Pricing is still to be defined, but we want to be clear that this
is a project whose main goal is not profit. We will keep pricing as low as
possible, and we will be transparent about our expenses.

## Looking ahead

We are very excited about what 2027 will bring. Thank you to all our users for
keeping our server bill high over the last two years, without any significant
updates from us.

See you in the skies.
