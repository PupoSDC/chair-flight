It has been quiet around here. For roughly two years Chair Flight sat untouched,
with no new development being made. Nonetheless, usage statistics (and server
bills!) show that people are still using our platform. As long as that holds
true, Chair Flight deserves to be worked on. And we are here to share with you
just that: we are building again, and there is a lot we want to ship!

## What has changed

We have already done some housekeeping. We brought the code up to date with
modern technologies and moved our backend from Neon to Supabase, which gives us
room to grow and makes some much bigger ideas possible. One of the main
motivations to restart development was curtailing server costs. Our Neon
database has slowly been costing us a few more cents every month, until it
reached roughly 50 euros of monthly usage. We don't really track user data, but
this indicates that more and more people are coming to Chair Flight. The move to
Supabase will allow us to reduce server usage a bit, but also build newer and
more advanced features.

We also took the chance to redesign the website from top to bottom. It looks
fresher, and we have retired some of the older artwork that had been decorating
Chair Flight for far too long. The flashcards module has been temporarily
removed. The 737 and the A320 modules are now independent. A new test maker has
been made. The Learning Objectives Table has been redone from scratch. And a few
more improvements here and there.

## What is coming: user progress

The feature we are most excited about is what we call user progress. You will be
able to create an account, build tests and keep track of how you are doing. Your
tests will follow you across devices and your statistics will be there for you
to analyse. You can join the discussion by commenting and replying, and even
contribute to the Chair Flight question banks straight from the app, no coding
required.

This will bring Chair Flight more in line with what you expect a question bank
to be.

## A note on AI

AI tools are now part of how we work. Most of this refactor was done with the
help of Claude, and our new artwork was created with ChatGPT. We believe these
tools let us keep up with a market that moves quickly, without compromising on
quality. Chair Flight is still run and built by volunteers, and they oversee
every change that goes in.

## Free and open source

Our mission is the same as ever. Chair Flight remains **free and open source**,
with all questions and content yours to browse, download and study at no cost.

That said, storing user data remotely costs money, and those bills will only
grow. To help cover them, we are working towards a subscription for the features
tied to user progress. We have not settled on a price yet, and profit is
explicitly not a goal.

Nonetheless, we want to be transparent that some of the features we will be
working on will be behind a paywall. Content, especially user-generated content,
will never be behind a paywall. You will always be able to search questions,
read documents, and create tests. But the more advanced features won't be
available "for free".

## Looking ahead

We are working towards making 2027 the biggest year in Chair Flight's history.
We are looking forward to hearing your feedback and building on it.

See you in the skies.
