## Buffet-onset chart practice

The [EASA ECQB Sample Annexes v3](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/downloads/143069/en#page=42)
include a clean-configuration buffet-onset boundary chart on page 42.

For the worked example at **FL350** and **120 t**, follow the **1 g** line
across to the FL350 curve. The two intersections give the low-speed buffet
boundary at **Mach 0.60** and the high-speed boundary at **Mach 0.84**.
At **Mach 0.80**, the boundary is about **1.5 g**, corresponding to about
**48°** of bank in a level coordinated turn.

Use the EASA sample to practise reading both Mach boundaries. The interactive
chart reproduces the low-speed limit and the load-factor example to chart
precision. Its plotted range ends near the high-speed limit in this example,
so it does not resolve that crossing.
